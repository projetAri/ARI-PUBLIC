### Purpose

Publication 001 establishes the public foundation of ARI.

> ARI should not merely accumulate information. It should construct, test, and revise relationships between information.

**Event → Experience → Memory → Associations → Hypothesis → Test → Evidence → Validation → Knowledge → Action → New Event**

### Public claims

Publication 001 presents ARI as a governed experimental architecture for studying experience as a first-class object, persistent associative memory, provenance and lineage, authority, conflict handling, hypothesis formation, evidence, validation, and revision.

### Claims intentionally not made

Publication 001 does not claim AGI, consciousness, unrestricted self-improvement, autonomous general-purpose continual learning, or demonstrated superiority over existing memory architectures.

### Private/public boundary

The public publication does not contain the private ALPHA repository, production databases, internal snapshots, protected experimental payloads, private operational mechanisms, credentials, or machine-specific material.
